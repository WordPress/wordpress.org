Upon successful submission of a job to the site, a unique job token is provided to the job poster. We emphatically implore job posters (multiple times) to make note of the job token. Provide this job token below in order to have the associated job immediately removed from the site.

If you don’t have the job token, you can contact us to request closure of the job. You must submit your request from an email address you provided via the job submission form. It would also be helpful if you reference the job by link and/or title, especially if you have more than one job in the system. As stated elsewhere, requests made via the contact page could take as much as 72 hours or longer to fulfill.

Reminder: All jobs are automatically removed from the site after 21 days. Only use the form below or contact us if you wish to have your job removed sooner than that.
